About Bagvera

A gentle guide to living with beautiful bags.

Bagvera is an independent site for people who love handbags and want them to last.

We write about colour, care and storage: the everyday decisions that make a good bag look good for years. Our tools help you choose a colour, plan your storage and rotate the bags you own.

“The most sustainable bag is the one you already own, looked after well.”

  • Independent: we do not sell bags or accept paid placements.
  • Practical: advice you can use with what you already have.
  • Reviewed: every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.
