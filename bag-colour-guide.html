The Bag Colour Guide

The right colour turns one bag into many outfits. Here is how to build a small, hard-working palette.

Start with one neutral

Your first bag should be a neutral that suits most of what you wear. Black is the classic, but if your wardrobe leans warm (camel, brown, cream), a cognac or tan bag often looks richer and goes with more.

The neutral palette

ColourWorks withFeel
BlackAlmost everything, especially cool tonesSharp, formal
CognacDenim, navy, cream, oliveWarm, relaxed
TanLight colours, summer clothesFresh, casual
TaupeGreys, pastels, black and whiteSoft, understated
CreamSpring and summer outfitsLight, elegant

Adding an accent colour

Once you have a neutral, a colour bag can lift simple outfits. Deep shades such as burgundy, navy and olive behave almost like neutrals. Brighter colours work best when your clothes are plain, letting the bag be the one point of colour.

To match or not to match

  • Match shoes and bag: polished and classic; good for formal occasions.
  • Echo a colour in your outfit: pick up a shade from a scarf or print.
  • Contrast deliberately: a cognac bag with black, or a red bag with navy.

Seasonal colours

Lighter bags (cream, tan, pastels) feel natural in spring and summer; deeper bags (burgundy, chocolate, black) suit autumn and winter. If you own one bag, choose a colour that works across seasons, such as taupe or cognac.
