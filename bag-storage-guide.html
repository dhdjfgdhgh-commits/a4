The Bag Storage Guide

Most handbag damage happens in storage, not in use. A few simple habits keep bags in shape for years.

Why storage matters

Leather is a natural material that reacts to light, heat, humidity and pressure. A bag squashed in a drawer or left in sunlight can crease, fade or lose its shape, while one stored well can look nearly new after many years.

The ideal spot

  • A shelf or cupboard with space between bags.
  • Out of direct sunlight, which fades colour.
  • Away from radiators and damp walls.
  • At room temperature with steady humidity.

Dust bags and stuffing

Store each bag in a breathable cotton or flannel dust bag; a clean pillowcase works too. Stuff the body lightly with acid-free tissue or a purpose-made insert so it keeps its shape. Avoid newspaper, which can transfer ink.

Storage by space

SpaceBest approach
Open shelfStand bags upright with dividers; use dust bags against light
WardrobeShelf inserts or clear boxes; avoid long-term hanging
DrawersSmall bags only, laid flat and stuffed, never stacked
Under-bed boxesBreathable boxes, one bag each, for seasonal storage

Hardware, straps and chains

Wrap metal chains and buckles in tissue so they do not scratch the leather. Detach long straps and store them flat or loosely coiled inside the bag.

Bringing a bag back into use

After long storage, air the bag for a day, wipe it with a soft cloth and condition smooth leather lightly if it feels dry. If a bag has lost its shape, stuff it firmly and leave it for a few days; a leather specialist can help with deeper creases.
